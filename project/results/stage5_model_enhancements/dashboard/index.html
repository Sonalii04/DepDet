Validation Decision Guardrails & Rationale

Mandatory Guardrails: Severe Recall ≥ 20.0% and Severe Precision ≥ 10.0%.

Key Finding: Naive argmax yields only ~5–12% severe recall. Sweeping operating thresholds enables clinicians to dial in screening sensitivity (e.g. 35% recall) without model retraining.

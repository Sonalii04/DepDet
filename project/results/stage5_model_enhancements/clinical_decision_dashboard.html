Core Architecture Breakdown

How Longitudinal Analysis Works In This Model

Traditional depression diagnostics occur intermittently via quarterly PHQ-9 questionnaires, leaving vast "blind spots" where relapses occur undetected. Our framework uses passive continuous wearable sensors (actigraphy, sleep fragmentation, circadian shifts) combined with a Recurrent Fusion GRU to detect subtle behavioral deterioration before severe clinical escalation.

Model Interpretability & Audit Trail

TreeSHAP: The Mathematical "Receipt" Behind Every Decision

In complex machine learning models (like XGBoost with 150 features across hundreds of decision trees), clinicians cannot blindly trust a black-box probability. SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) calculates the exact fair mathematical contribution of each feature using cooperative game theory:

Base Population Risk (4.6%) + Positive Pushes (+ Risk Factors) - Protective Buffers (- Resilience) = Patient's Final Severe Risk
